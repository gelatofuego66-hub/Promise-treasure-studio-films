# Free Studio v2

Works without subscription: animated scene preview and silent WebM export, browser text-to-speech dialogue, separate microphone audio recording, character image uploads, screenplay storage.

This is NOT photorealistic AI video or automatic lip-sync. A GPU and AI models are needed for those features.

Open-source model options:
Wan2.2 video https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2
Kokoro voice https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro

Upload all website files to an HTTPS host. Use Add to Home Screen on Android Chrome.
